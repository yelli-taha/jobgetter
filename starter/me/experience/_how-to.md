# Experience files

One file per job, internship, volunteer role or project, named by a short key (e.g. `acme.md`). The same key
marks the job in the master resume (`% JOB: acme`). Setup creates these from the resume and interview.

Template for each file:

# <Employer>
- Key: <short key, same as in the master resume>
- Title:
- Location:
- Dates: <Mon YYYY> - <Mon YYYY or Present> (exactly as on the resume)
- Type: full-time / part-time / internship / volunteer / freelance

## What the company does
## What I did (facts, numbers, tools; more than fits on the resume, so Claude can choose per job)
## Resume bullets (current version)
## Results and numbers I can prove
## Tools used here
## Rules for using this experience (e.g. always include; only for sales roles; keep it short)
