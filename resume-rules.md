# Resume Rules

## Format
- Exactly one page (me/profile.json resume.max_pages).
- The master is me/samples/master-resume.tex (LaTeX). Each job gets a copy with CONTENT changes only: the layout,
  fonts, margins and commands never change. Compiled with pdflatex (same as Overleaf), saved as .tex and .pdf
  named by company and role, so I can open the .tex in Overleaf if I want.
- Escape LaTeX special characters in new text: & as \&, % as \%, $ as \$, # as \#, _ as \_.
- Never edit or create a PDF directly.

## Facts
- Dates, titles, employers and numbers exactly as in me/experience/. Never change them to fit a role.
- Never invent experience, tools, numbers, results, skill levels or certificates (only what skills.md lists).

## What goes on each resume is decided by the job
- No fixed ranking of jobs or bullets. For each posting choose what to include, what goes first and what to cut.
- Always kept: Education, and the jobs listed in profile.json resume.always_keep.
- Jobs stay in date order (newest first). Bullets can be reordered, reworded (same facts) or swapped for others from
  my experience files. Mirror the posting's words where they honestly describe what I did.
- Skills table: tools that matter for this job first; the always-included skills plus 2-3 picked for this posting.
- Over one page: cut what matters least for THIS job, and list each cut and why on the dashboard.

## Never
(Setup and "save this to the rules" add my own never-rules here, e.g. a skill I don't want listed.)

## Before finishing
- One page, every fact checked against me/, changes from the master listed on the dashboard.
